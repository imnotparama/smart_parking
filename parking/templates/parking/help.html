Support & Guide

Everything you need to know about using Smart Parking Analysis. Follow our step-by-step guide to master the platform.

How It Works

Step 1

Find a Slot

Head to the Dashboard. Available spots glow in Green. Use the intuitive layout to locate your ideal space instantly.

Step 2

Reserve Instantly

Click any available slot to open the booking panel. Input your vehicle details, select your duration, and secure your spot with a single click.

Step 3

Manage Your Fleet

Access My Bookings to track active sessions or review history. Active spots illuminate in Yellow on the live grid.

Step 4

Track Insights

Visit My Analytics to visualize your parking expenditure and engagement metrics through interactive, premium charts.
