Support & Guide

Everything you need to know about using Smart Parking Analysis.

How It Works

Step 1

Find a Slot

Head to the Home page. Available parking slots illuminate in glowing Green. Use the search bar or filters to quickly locate the perfect spot.

Step 2

Reserve Instantly

Click a green slot. The Booking Panel opens instantly. Enter your vehicle info, choose your timeframe, pick a payment method, and hit Select a Slot to secure it.

Step 3

Manage Fleet

Visit My Bookings to view your active and historical sessions. Active slots light up in Blue on the layout, making locating your vehicle a breeze.

Step 4

Track Insights

Unlock My Analytics for visualized data on your parking history, expenditures, and platform usage over time via beautiful, interactive charts.
