How to Use Smart Parking

Welcome to Smart Parking Analysis! Follow this quick guide to manage your parking needs seamlessly.

1 Find an Available Slot

Go to the Home page. You will see a grid of all parking slots. Look for slots highlighted in Green (Available). Occupied slots are Grey. You can use the search bar to find a specific slot or filter to show only available ones.

2 Make a Booking

Click on any Green available slot. The Booking Details panel on the right will update. Enter your Vehicle Number, select the Date, Time, and Duration, then pick a Payment Method. Finally, click Select a Slot to confirm your booking.

3 Manage Your Bookings

Navigate to the My Bookings page to view your active and past reservations. Active bookings are highlighted in Blue on the home layout so you can easily find your car.

4 View Analytics

Check out My Analytics to see your parking history, total spending, and usage patterns over time. This helps you track your parking habits and expenses.
